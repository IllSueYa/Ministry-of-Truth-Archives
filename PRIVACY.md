# Privacy Policy — Ministry of Truth Archives

**Effective date:** August 30, 2026

Ministry of Truth Archives ("the Bot", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our") is an unofficial community-created Discord application that provides a searchable Helldivers 2 lore encyclopedia through Discord application commands.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Bot accesses, how that information is used, what is retained, and how users can request deletion of information associated with the Bot.

## 1. Information the Bot Processes

When you interact with Ministry of Truth Archives through Discord, Discord may provide information necessary to deliver and process the interaction. Depending on the context of the interaction, this may include:

- Your Discord user ID and basic account information supplied with an interaction.
- The Discord server (guild) ID where a server interaction occurs.
- Channel and interaction identifiers required to receive and respond to a command.
- The application command name and options you submit, such as the topic entered into `/lore`.
- Technical information supplied by Discord that is necessary to process the interaction.

The Bot is designed around Discord application commands and does not require general access to ordinary server conversations to provide its core lore-search functionality.

## 2. Information We Intentionally Store

The current core functionality of Ministry of Truth Archives does **not intentionally maintain a persistent database of Discord user profiles, ordinary Discord messages, or users' `/lore` search history**.

The Bot maintains its own encyclopedia data, application configuration, and operational files necessary to provide the service. Encyclopedia content is not user profile data.

Hosting infrastructure may temporarily process or retain operational information, such as application logs, for reliability, troubleshooting, security, or abuse prevention. Such logs may contain technical identifiers supplied during Discord interactions.

We do not use Discord API data to build advertising profiles or sell personal information.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information received from Discord is used only as reasonably necessary to:

- Receive and respond to application commands.
- Return requested encyclopedia information.
- Provide autocomplete and search functionality.
- Operate, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot the Bot.
- Diagnose errors and prevent abuse.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations and Discord requirements.

Discord API data is not used for unrelated profiling, advertising, or eligibility decisions.

## 4. Message Content

Ministry of Truth Archives is designed to use application commands such as `/lore`.

Its core encyclopedia functionality does not require the Bot to monitor or archive ordinary messages sent by users in Discord servers.

If future features require access to additional Discord data, including message content, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate before that data is used for the new functionality.

## 5. Data Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell Discord user data.

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the Bot, such as hosting or infrastructure providers, solely as necessary to provide and maintain the service.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect the security and integrity of the Bot and its users.

Discord separately processes information according to Discord's own policies and terms.

## 6. Data Retention

Discord interaction data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's functionality, troubleshoot operational issues, maintain security, or satisfy applicable legal requirements.

The Bot does not intentionally maintain a permanent history of individual `/lore` searches as part of its core functionality.

Temporary operational logs may be automatically removed or rotated by the hosting environment.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was processed, it should be deleted or allowed to expire unless retention is required by law.

## 7. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of personal information specifically retained by Ministry of Truth Archives.

Until a dedicated support channel is published, requests may be submitted through the project's official GitHub repository:

https://github.com/IllSueYa/Ministry-of-Truth-Archives

A deletion request should identify the Discord account concerned and provide enough information to reasonably verify and locate data associated with the request. Do **not** publicly post passwords, authentication tokens, or other sensitive credentials.

If the Bot does not retain personal information associated with the requesting user, there may be no stored data to delete.

Deleting data controlled independently by Discord must be handled through Discord.

## 8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the Bot and information it processes.

Bot tokens, credentials, and other secrets are not intended to be included in the public source repository. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Children

Ministry of Truth Archives is intended for use through Discord and is not directed to children below the minimum age permitted to use Discord in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly use the Bot to solicit personal information from children.

## 10. Third-Party Links and Sources

Lore entries may contain links to third-party resources, including the community-maintained Helldivers Wiki.

Those websites and services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Ministry of Truth Archives is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Ministry of Truth Archives changes.

Material changes to the Bot's data practices should be reflected in an updated version of this policy. The effective date at the top of the document will indicate the current version.

Continued use of the Bot after an updated policy becomes effective is subject to the updated policy and applicable law.
